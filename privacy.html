Privacy Policy

OopsiePixels Automation

Last updated: November 10, 2026

1. Overview

OopsiePixels Automation is a private content management tool designed to help authorized operators manage and publish original gaming videos to connected social media accounts, including TikTok.

2. Information We Process

When an account is connected, the application may process account identifiers, profile information, authorization tokens, video metadata, and media files necessary for publishing.

3. How Information Is Used

Information is used to authenticate authorized accounts, prepare video content, publish approved videos, and track publishing results.

4. Data Storage and Security

Application data may be processed and stored in access-controlled automation infrastructure. Authorization credentials are intended to be kept confidential and are not published in public repositories.

5. Data Sharing

Information is transmitted to connected social media platforms as necessary to perform authorized publishing actions. We do not sell personal information.

6. Data Retention

Account connection information and publishing records are retained only as needed for the operation of the service. Data may be deleted when an account is disconnected or the service is discontinued, subject to applicable requirements.

7. Account Disconnection and Data Requests

Authorized users may revoke application access through the relevant social media platform's settings. Requests concerning stored application data may be directed to the application operator.

8. Third-Party Services

Connected platforms, including TikTok, process information under their own privacy policies.

9. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when application functionality or data practices change.

10. Contact

For privacy-related inquiries, contact the OopsiePixels account owner through the official OopsiePixels YouTube channel.
